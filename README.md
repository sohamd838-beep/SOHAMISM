What is Sohamism? The 5555 philosophy, explained in one page
Sohamism is a modern, non-theistic personal philosophy created by Soham Deshmukh in 2026. Its code is 5555, and the whole thing fits in one sentence: change is inevitable, becoming is a choice. The full creed is at sohamism.netlify.app
I wrote it because I kept meeting people who were waiting. Waiting to feel ready, waiting for permission, waiting for the version of themselves that would finally start. Sohamism has one sin — drift — and one sacred act — choosing your next shape.
It is organised as four fives.
5 — five truths. Everything changes. You are not your present shape. Change happens to you; becoming is done by you. What you release returns as room. No one becomes alone.
55 — five practices. First Light, five minutes before you touch your phone. The Turning Breath: in for five, hold for five, out for five, rest for five. The Unmaking Hour, once a week, removing one thing that no longer fits. The Witness, telling one person what you are becoming. The Small Becoming, doing one thing today you could not do last year.
555 — five vows, said aloud once to one witness. There is no officiant, because there is no hierarchy.
5555 — the fivefold year. Four turnings of the sun, and a fifth day that belongs to you.
There is no god in it, no afterlife, and no fee. You cannot be excommunicated — leaving is a change, and the creed is in favour of changes. There is a hymn, "Sky's My Floor", and a public register of everyone who has signed it.
